03 / Litbuy shopping guide

Build a shortlist.
Then earn your yes.

Useful Litbuy finds need more than an attractive photo. Keep comparable details together so you can see which items meet your needs and which still need research.

A simple spreadsheet can separate what the listing states, what you have checked and what remains unknown.

  1. 01

    Choose a specific use

    Start with a daily sneaker, a layering hoodie or a small accessory. Define your preferred fit, color and budget before collecting links.

  2. 02

    Record the current item details

    Save the listing URL, seller name, selected variant, listed price and the date you checked it. Mark missing details as unknown.

  3. 03

    Resolve fit and material questions

    Compare the size chart with your own measurements. Ask for clarification when materials, dimensions or return terms are unclear.

  4. 04

    Compare the complete purchase cost

    Check delivery, service fees and applicable import charges. Revisit the live listing before paying because prices and availability can change.

04 / The comparison desk

Make each row useful.

A Litbuy clothing spreadsheet and a Litbuy sneaker spreadsheet need different fit details. Keep the essentials consistent across both.

Suggested fields for your fashion research spreadsheet
Field What to record What it helps you check
Item & variant Listing URL, model, color and selected size Whether you are comparing the same configuration
Clothing fit Chest, length, shoulder, waist or inseam as relevant How the garment compares with clothing you own
Sneaker fit Seller size chart and foot-length guidance where available Whether the stated sizing matches your measurements
Materials Stated fabric composition, leather type, metal or plating Whether the listing provides enough material detail
Seller & terms Seller identity, supporting information and return conditions What evidence and purchase protections are available
Cost & status Currency, item price, charges, availability and check date Which numbers are current and which are estimates

Clear answers to common questions about spreadsheets, fit and purchase research.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product-research list associated with shopping through Litbuy. It may organize item links, categories, prices and notes. A spreadsheet is a starting point for research, not proof of stock, authenticity or seller reliability.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It is not operated by or affiliated with Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

How should I use a Litbuy clothing spreadsheet?

Choose a clothing category, open the current listing and compare its measurements with a garment you already own. Record the size chart, fabric information, selected variant and return conditions before deciding.

What should I check in a Litbuy sneaker spreadsheet?

Check the exact model, color, seller size chart, stated materials and current item details. Compare foot-length guidance where provided. A familiar size label or product photograph alone does not establish fit or authenticity.

Does a listed price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, any service or payment fees, international shipping and applicable import charges. Availability and charges depend on the seller, service and destination.

Are the pictured branded items guaranteed to be available on CAFANS?

No. The images are editorial style references. Category links lead to CAFANS browsing pages, not verified listings for the exact pictured items. Confirm availability, provenance and authenticity independently before purchasing.
